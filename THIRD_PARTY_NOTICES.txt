Equipment recommendation discovery index:
KQM original character guides, checked 2026-10-16.
https://keqingmains.com/
Each character's exact guide URL is included in recommendations.js and linked
beside its equipment controls. Only concise equipment names and independently
written scope notes are included; guide prose and graphics are not
reproduced. This index is for finding commonly discussed equipment across builds,
not a computed ranking for the current configuration. Mixed two-piece examples
are excluded from four-piece recommendations. Dedicated weapons and associated
weapons are labelled separately. All other catalog equipment remains available.
